# Support / Destek

## Turkce

Teknik destek ve yazilimla ilgili sorular icin:

**support@turkuazlabs.com**

Genel iletisim, is birligi ve diger konular icin:

**info@turkuazlabs.com**

Guvenlik aciklari icin herkese acik issue acmayin. Bunun yerine:

**security@turkuazlabs.com**

Bir hata bildirirken mumkunse proje adi, surum, isletim sistemi, tekrar uretme adimlari ve ilgili loglari ekleyin.

## English

For technical support and software-related questions:

**support@turkuazlabs.com**

For general inquiries and collaboration:

**info@turkuazlabs.com**

For security vulnerabilities, do not open a public issue. Contact:

**security@turkuazlabs.com**

When reporting a problem, please include the project name, version, operating system, reproduction steps, and relevant logs when possible.
